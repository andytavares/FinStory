# Categories

![Categories](img/categories.png)

**Categories** shows every category as a card, with its subcategories and how much you spent. The header shows the number of groups and subcategories. Money comes with built-in categories. You can add your own. Subcategories go one level deep.

## Add a category

1. Click **+ New category**.
2. Type a name.
3. Click **Add**, or press **Return**.

## Add a subcategory

1. On the group's card, click **+ Subcategory**.
2. Type a name.
3. Click **Add**.

## Rename a category

1. Click **⋯** next to the category. Subcategory buttons appear when you hover over the row.
2. Click **Rename**.
3. Type the new name and press **Return**.

Renaming changes the display name only.

## Merge two categories

1. Click **⋯**, then **Merge**.
2. Choose the category to merge into in **Merge into…**.

Transactions move to the other category. The merged category disappears from the list.

## Hide a category

Click **⋯**, then **Hide**. A hidden category cannot be given to new transactions. Past transactions keep it, so old totals still add up. To bring it back, click **⋯**, then **Show**.

## Delete a category

1. Click **⋯**, then **Delete**.
2. If transactions or subcategories use it, Money asks "Where should they go?". Choose a category.
3. Click **Move and delete**.

If nothing uses the category, it is deleted at once.
