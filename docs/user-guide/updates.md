# Updates

Money updates itself.

## How it works

- Money checks for a new version when it opens, and every 6 hours while it runs.
- Money downloads a new version in the background.
- A message appears at the bottom right: "Money <version> is ready".

## Install an update

1. Click **Restart now**. Money closes and opens again on the new version.
2. Or click **Later**. Money installs the update the next time you quit and open it.

## Where updates come from

Money gets updates from the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/andytavares/FinStory/releases). Each version there is signed and notarized by Apple. A new version appears to Money only after it is published as a release, not as a draft.

## Check your version

Open **Settings**, then **Updates**. Or open **Health** and read the **Updates** tile.

## If updates do not arrive

- Check your internet connection.
- Open the Releases page. Check that a newer version is published.
- Download the newest `.dmg` and install it over the old copy. Your data stays.
